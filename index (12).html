    How to enter trades
    Before a trade: type contracts and the premium you paid to see cost, stop and target. Nothing is logged yet. After it closes: type what you sold for (total), the sell price per contract, or the profit/loss, whichever is picked above, and press Enter. The app works out the real profit. Quick line: TSLA 2 @0.50 =200 (sold for $200), TSLA 2 @0.50 s1.00 (sold at $1.00 each), TSLA 2 -80p (profit/loss). Chips always log a profit or loss sized to your risk.

    Import, export & backup

    Import from your broker
    Where to find the file at your broker
    Look for History, Activity or Statements, pick a date range, and choose Export or Download as CSV. Menu names change over time; these are the usual places:
    • Robinhood: Account → Reports and statements → Reports → account activity (CSV)
    • Webull: desktop app → Orders → order history → Export
    • Schwab: Accounts → History → Export
    • Fidelity: Accounts & Trade → Activity & Orders → Download
    • Interactive Brokers: Performance & Reports → Statements → Activity, format CSV
    • tastytrade: History → Transactions → CSV
    Other brokers usually work too. If the columns aren't recognised, you'll be asked to match them.

    Tell the app which column holds what (hold Ctrl or ⌘ to pick more than one), then read the file again.
